Gary Gaydon

Faith. Failure. Grace. Purpose.

My Story

Founder of No Judgement

"My Dad taught me how to fight. My Mom taught me why to fight."

A Promise Before I Was Born

Before I was ever born, my mother prayed a simple prayer.

After raising three daughters, she asked God for a son and promised that if He answered her prayer, she would dedicate that child back to Him.

On January 1, 1978, I was born.

Growing up, my mother reminded me of that prayer often. Because of it, I always felt drawn toward God. I never fully understood His plan, but I always believed there was a purpose for my life.

Family was everything in our home.

Those values stayed with me through every victory, every failure, every loss, and every lesson.

From The Country To The Streets

I spent the early part of my childhood in the country where life was peaceful.

Everything changed when I was twelve years old and my family moved to a one-lane trailer park in a rough neighborhood.

The first night we were there, I watched a woman chase a man out of a trailer with a butcher knife.

I was shocked.

I had never seen anything like it before.

"I'm going to have to fight."

What I didn't realize was that the greatest battles ahead of me would not be physical.

They would be spiritual.

The Crime That Changed Everything

At seventeen years old, a group of my friends and I decided to rob a drug dealer we believed was selling drugs in our area.

We thought we were simply putting him out of business.

Instead, we got caught.

The first plea bargain offered to me was thirty-nine years.

My mother looked at me and said:

"Get in the car."

She took me and my girlfriend to New York to stay with my uncle in the Adirondack Mountains.

I stayed there almost a year before one routine traffic stop changed everything.

The Prayer That Changed My Life

After my arrest, I found myself sitting in a jail cell beside a man who had burned his entire family alive.

I still remember the darkness in his eyes.

That night I got down on my knees and prayed.

"Lord, I know Your Word says that if I ask forgiveness and ask Jesus into my heart that You will forgive me. I can't promise I won't fail. I can't promise I won't sin. I'm just asking You to forgive me, come into my heart, and help me live for You."

That night, for the first and only time in my life, I heard the Lord speak.

"You are forgiven."

February 3, 1997 changed my life forever.

The Sign

After that prayer, I asked God for one thing.

Not freedom.

Not a lighter sentence.

Just a sign that He would never leave me nor forsake me.

Looking through the small jail window, I watched a shooting star cross the sky.

To me, that was God's answer.

A peace came over me unlike anything I had ever experienced.

Six Years Of Preparation

Before I left for prison, my father handed me a small red Bible.

I still have it today.

It is worn out, marked up, torn, and held together by years of use.

Before I left he told me:

"Be a fisherman of men."

At the time I thought prison was punishment.

Today I believe it was preparation.

The judge sentenced me to six years.

Looking back now, I believe God knew I needed all six.

The Vision

While I was in prison, God gave me a vision.

I saw a church shaped like a cross.

At the center was the body where people gathered together in worship and fellowship.

One side was recovery and restoration.

The other side was training and preparation.

At the foot of the cross, the Gospel was taken out into the community.

At the head of the cross was a twenty-four-hour prayer room.

Prayer never stopped.

Hope never stopped.

Grace never stopped.

At the time I did not fully understand what God was showing me.

Today I believe He was giving me a glimpse of what would eventually become No Judgement.

The Fall

After prison, I dedicated myself to ministry.

I became ordained.

I preached.

I served.

Then life happened.

I lost my mother.

I lost my father.

I lost two sisters.

The pain became overwhelming.

Instead of turning toward God, I turned toward alcohol.

Alcohol became my comfort.

Then alcohol became my prison.

Where Is Gary?

"Where is Gary?"

People started asking that question because I would disappear for days drinking.

The truth is, I couldn't find myself.

I was drinking half a gallon of vodka a day.

I lost jobs.

I overdosed.

I woke up in hospitals.

There were moments when I did not even want to live.

The Sister Who Never Quit

There is another reason I am standing here today.

My sister.

When everyone else saw an addict, she still saw her brother.

When I stopped believing in myself, she kept believing in me.

When I had given up hope, she kept praying.

"If it were not for my sister, I would have given up."

God used her faith, her love, and her persistence to help carry me through one of the darkest seasons of my life.

Recovery

Eventually I entered treatment again.

This time I wasn't doing it for anyone else.

I was doing it for me.

"You can't love someone else if you can't love yourself."

For the first time, I truly understood.

You have to believe you are worth fighting for.

God Knew Where I Was

People often talk about Adam hiding in the Garden.

"Adam, where are you?"

God knew where Adam was.

Adam was the one hiding.

For years, I was Adam.

People asked:

"Where is Gary?"

The truth is, I was hiding.

But God knew where I was the entire time.

He never stopped calling.

He never stopped loving me.

He never stopped pursuing me.

Why No Judgement Exists

No Judgement exists because I have experienced the grace of God.

Not because I earned it.

Not because I deserved it.

Because God refused to give up on me.

I have seen people treated like numbers.

I have seen recovery become an industry instead of a family.

That is not what No Judgement is about.

"You are not alone."

I want us to eat together.

Work together.

Pray together.

Heal together.

Grow together.

More Than Conquerors

Everything I have been through prepared me for this mission.

The prison.

The ministry.

The addiction.

The recovery.

The grace.

All of it.

"You are worth the fight."

Come heal with us.

Come grow with us.

Come build with us.

Come discover your purpose with us.

Your story is not over.

"If it were not for the grace of God, I would not be here today."

Gary Gaydon
Founder, No Judgement

Through Christ, we are more than conquerors.
