Volunteer With Us

Changing communities one act of service at a time.

Why Volunteers Matter

Volunteers are the heart of every successful community. They bring skills, experience, compassion, and a willingness to help others.

No Judgement believes meaningful community change happens when people step forward and choose to serve. Every hour volunteered creates opportunities, connections, and positive outcomes that strengthen the entire community.

Join The Movement

Whether you can give a few hours a month or become actively involved in community projects, your time and talent can help make a difference.

We welcome individuals, businesses, churches, students, retirees, community organizations and families who want to be part of something bigger than themselves.

Volunteer Opportunities

🤝 Community Outreach

Help support local outreach events, resource fairs and community engagement projects.

🌱 Recovery Support

Assist with recovery-friendly initiatives, resource navigation and peer support efforts.

🏡 Family Programs

Support family-centered activities, community events and educational programs.

🎉 Special Events

Help organize, coordinate and support community gatherings and service projects.

📚 Administrative Support

Assist with organization, planning, communications and volunteer coordination.

💼 Professional Skills

Share expertise in marketing, technology, education, trades, business or leadership.

Volunteer Database

No Judgement is actively building a volunteer network to connect people with opportunities to serve. As the organization grows, volunteers will play a key role in outreach, programs, events and community projects.

Future volunteer portal features may include volunteer profiles, project assignments, event registration, service-hour tracking and community impact reporting.
